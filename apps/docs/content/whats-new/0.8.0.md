- The dashboard shell: `AppShell` with `Sidebar` and `TopBar`, `AppPage`, `PageHeader` and tabs, built to the page grammar in the layout rules.
- Data and charts: `DataTable` with saved views, filters and column settings, `MetricCard`, `StatTabs` and in-house line, area and bar charts.
- Settings, search and panels: `SettingsPage` and its rows, `CommandSearch` with typed sources and filters, `SearchResults`, `SidePanel` and the form, consequence and full-screen dialogs.
