- `@butteryui/core`, `@butteryui/react`, `@butteryui/test` and `@butteryui/lint` are on npm, released together at one version, and every docs page is Markdown at its path plus `.md`, with `llms.txt` listing them all for coding agents.
- `Sheet`, `Accordion`, `Faq`, `ContextMenu` and the `Catalog` parts are new, and fields, tabs, badges, cards, tables and a dozen more take `variant`, `size`, `shape` or `layout` options, each defaulting to the look they already had.
- `expectButtery` and `LayoutGuard` check three more things: text crowding the line under it, a word broken across lines, and controls that sit off the line beside them; `checkButtery` returns what they find instead of failing. `pnpm new-app` starts from the dashboard, editor or marketing template, which the new demos, Deckhand and the Halfmoon shop, are built on.
