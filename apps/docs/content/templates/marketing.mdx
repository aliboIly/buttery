The marketing template is a one-page site: a sticky bar whose links scroll to their sections, a hero, sections for what the product does, a few numbers and two plans, a closing call to action and a footer. Use it for a product's launch page or a small site that sells one thing.

<TemplatePreview name="marketing" />

## Start from it

<Steps>
<Step>

### Copy the template

```bash
pnpm new-app my-app --template marketing
```

This works inside the buttery repository today. It copies `apps/templates/marketing` to `apps/my-app`, gives the copy its name, which also replaces "buttery site" in the bar and the footer, and the next free port, then installs it.

Once `create-buttery` is published, this starts the same app in any folder:

```bash
npx create-buttery my-app --template marketing
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Start it

```bash
pnpm --filter my-app dev
```

Open the address it prints.

</Step>
</Steps>

> **Links to sections.** The bar's links and buttons go to sections of the page with `scrollToSection`, which stops each section below the sticky bar and leaves the address alone.

It's made from the [marketing page](/patterns/marketing-page) patterns, and its router is explained under [routing](/templates/dashboard#routing) on the dashboard's page.

## A demo made from it

The [Halfmoon shop](/demos/roastery-shop) is this template with Halfmoon Roastery's own store in place of the template's page: menus in the bar, a split hero with this week's coffee, coffees that open into their details, a subscription builder, and a bag that opens in a sheet from any page and checks out on pages of its own.
