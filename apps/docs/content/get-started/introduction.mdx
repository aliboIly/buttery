buttery is a React design system for web apps and Tauri desktop apps: tokens, layout primitives, components and motion that agree with each other, and checks that hold every screen to them. Use it when you want an app's spacing, type, surfaces and motion to agree without reviewing every screen by hand.

## Try it

The card is made of buttery's components, `Surface`, `SegmentedControl`, `Checkbox`, `Avatar`, `Switch` and `Button`, and every color, size, corner and gap in it is a token, so a new theme changes all of them at once. Pick a theme, a density, a brand color (the theme's `accent` and `primary`) and a corner style, then tick the tasks and press Launch. A nested `ButteryProvider` applies the picks to the card alone.

## What buttery gives an app

<FeatureGrid>
<Feature visual="tokens">

### Tokens and themes

Every color, size, corner, shadow, duration and layer is a token, a CSS variable with a typed name, with values for light and dark and for comfortable and compact density. A theme from `createTheme` sets the accent, the grays and the corners, and every component follows. [Foundations](/foundations/colors)

</Feature>
<Feature visual="layout">

### Layout that holds

Pages lay out with gaps instead of margins, controls take their heights from tokens, and long text truncates on purpose. A toolbar that runs out of room folds its last actions into More instead of wrapping, as the one on this card does when you narrow it. [The layout contract](/layout/the-layout-contract)

</Feature>
<Feature visual="motion">

### Motion with rules

Each kind of change has its own motion: a drag follows the pointer, a toggle springs, an indicator slides to its new place and a swapped value crossfades. With reduced motion, things fade or change at once. [Motion principles](/motion/principles)

</Feature>
<Feature visual="components">

### Components for whole apps

From `Button`, `Switch` and `Avatar` to `DataTable`, `CommandSearch` and `AppShell`, each with keyboard support, names for screen readers, examples and a page of its own. [All components](/components/overview)

</Feature>
<Feature visual="checks">

### Checks that say how to fix

`expectButtery` fails a Playwright test when anything overlaps, overflows, truncates without saying so, crowds the line under it or moves after a press, and says what to change. `LayoutGuard` outlines the same problems while you work. [Testing](/tools/testing)

</Feature>
<Feature visual="agents">

### Made for coding agents

Every page is Markdown at its address plus `.md`, and `llms.txt` lists them all. `AGENTS.md` and the make-it-buttery skill give a coding agent the rules, and every check's message says what to change. [Using coding agents](/get-started/using-coding-agents)

</Feature>
</FeatureGrid>
