buttery's motion follows ten rules, measured from apps that feel smooth to use. Use them to decide whether something should move at all, and how, before reaching for a preset.

## The ten rules

<Rules>
<Rule title="Direct manipulation is instant">

Dragging a slider or picking a swatch updates the result in the same frame. A value the person is driving is set, never tweened.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Discrete changes settle in about 250ms">

A change of state settles with no overshoot, on `spring.smooth`. Small selection indicators, such as a segmented control's pill, are quicker, on `spring.snappy`.

</Rule>
<Rule title="One surface morphs">

A surface changes shape instead of closing and reopening: a menu that switches sections resizes, and a card that opens becomes its dialog.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Containers follow their content">

A container animates its height when what's inside it changes, and the content crossfades.

</Rule>
<Rule title="New context dims">

Rather than blacking out, a menu over content blurs the page behind, and a dialog darkens it.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Arrivals are choreographed">

A hero or a grid enters with a short rise and fade, staggered about 50ms, in 900ms at most, once.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Exits are faster than entrances">

An exit takes about 60% as long as its entrance, because the person has already decided.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Hover answers within 150ms">

An arrow extends, a card lifts 2px. Nothing is shown only on hover.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Celebrations are rare">

Confetti fires only for a real finish, such as completing onboarding.

</Rule>
<Rule title="Reduced motion is a mode of its own">

Springs become instant, reveals become plain fades of 100ms at most, loops stop and confetti is off. Layout still updates.

</Rule>
</Rules>

## What never animates

- Numbers a person typed or set.
- Layout during a drag.
- Anything longer than 400ms, apart from choreographed entrances and ambient loops.

## CSS transitions

CSS transitions move only `opacity`, transforms, colors, shadows, `filter` and `backdrop-filter`. A change of size or position goes through Motion's `layout`.

<DoDont>
<Do>

```css
.chip {
  transition: background-color var(--bt-dur-fast) var(--bt-ease-out);
}
```

Transition the property that changes, on a token.

</Do>
<Dont>

```css
.chip {
  transition: all 120ms;
}
```

`transition: all` animates layout too, and buttery's stylelint config fails it.

</Dont>
</DoDont>
