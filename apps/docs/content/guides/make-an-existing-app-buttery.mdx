This guide moves an existing React app onto buttery without a rewrite: wire up the provider, check the layout, then convert a screen at a time from the top down. Follow it when an app already works and you want it to look, move and lay out like buttery.

## 1. Wire it up

Import `@buttery/core/styles.css` and `@buttery/react/styles.css` at the app's entry and wrap it in `ButteryProvider`. buttery's styles sit in CSS cascade layers, so a style the app sets itself still wins, but what the app leaves to the browser changes:

- The page's font becomes Inter, with `body` text at 14px on a 20px line, and the page's background and text take buttery's colors.
- A small reset applies: `box-sizing: border-box` on every element, no default margins on headings, paragraphs and lists, images and SVGs as blocks no wider than their container, and form controls that take their font from the page.
- Selected text takes buttery's selection color, whatever has keyboard focus shows buttery's focus ring, and scrollbars turn thin, in the border color.
- `ButteryProvider` follows the system's light or dark setting, which switches those colors. Pass `colorScheme="light"` until the app's own styles are ready for dark.

The app also gains buttery's tokens, the layers that menus, dialogs and toasts open into, and `LayoutGuard` in development.

## 2. See where it stands

Run the app in development and narrow the window from wide to a phone's width. `LayoutGuard` outlines what overlaps, overflows or truncates without saying so. Add one Playwright test per main page with `expectButtery(page, quickMatrix)`, and keep its report as the list of what to fix.

## 3. Convert a screen, top down

For one screen at a time:

- Match the whole screen to a pattern if one fits: `AppShell` with `AppPage`, a settings page, a detail page.
- Give each region a `Surface` level and lay out siblings with `Stack`, `Inline` and `Grid`, dropping margins for gaps.
- Swap each control for its buttery component: `Button`, `TextField`, `Select`, `Switch`.
- Wrap what has no buttery match in `buttery(Component, { surface: "card" })`, so it takes the surface and motion recipes.
- Replace raw colors, sizes, radii and durations with tokens. buttery's stylelint config lists the ones left.

## 4. Keep it there

Add `@buttery/lint`'s configs and the `expectButtery` tests to the app's checks, so a screen stays buttery once it's converted.
