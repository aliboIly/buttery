This page lists every buttery component in the group the sidebar gives it, with the line its own page starts with. Use it to find the component for a job: each name opens its page, with a live demo, examples and props.

The building blocks have sections of their own:

- `Stack`, `Inline`, `Grid` and `Surface` are in [Layout](/layout/stack-inline-and-grid).
- `Text` and `Icon` are in [Foundations](/foundations/typography).
- The motion presets are in [Motion](/motion/presets).
- Page-sized parts, such as `AppShell`, are in [Patterns](/patterns/app-shell).
