Each release has its notes here: its number and date, what it's for, examples of what it brought to try, its highlights and the components that arrived in it. Releases are numbered one minor version per phase of the build plan, from 0.1 to 0.9, and 1.0 is the first on npm. From then on, each release also lists every change from the packages' changelogs. The [Roadmap](/resources/roadmap) has what's coming.
