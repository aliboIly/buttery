Every release gets a short entry here: its name, its date and the two or three things it's for. A release still being built says so in its name, and its date is the day its entry last changed. Once buttery is on npm, each entry also lists every change from the packages' changelogs, which Changesets writes as a release is versioned. The [Roadmap](/resources/roadmap) has what's coming.
