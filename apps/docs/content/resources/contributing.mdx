buttery is a pnpm monorepo: the packages under `packages/`, this site and the starter under `apps/`. This page covers what a change needs before it's ready; `AGENTS.md` in the repository has the full rules, for people and coding agents alike.

## The rules a change follows

- Tokens only: no raw colors, sizes, radii, shadows, durations or z-indexes outside the token source.
- The layout contract: flow layout with gaps, no outer margins, sizes from tokens, container queries.
- The component contract: `ref` as a prop, controlled and uncontrolled, one prop vocabulary, JSDoc on every export and prop, keyboard and screen reader support, works with motion off.
- The motion rules: direct manipulation is instant, discrete changes spring, exits are faster than entrances.
- Docs in the same change: a component's page, examples and metadata change with it, and the docs check fails when they don't.

## Commits

One focused change per commit, with a conventional prefix such as `feat(react):` or `fix(core):` and a subject of 72 characters at most, in the imperative.
