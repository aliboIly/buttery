The form patterns are complete sign-in and sign-up forms: the fields at their large size, a full-width submit, other ways in, and errors summed up at the top with links to the fields that have them. Use them as they are for an app's account screens, and their parts as a model for any form.

## How a buttery form behaves

Fields check themselves when a person leaves them, not on every key. Submitting a form with errors puts a summary at its top, says how many there are, and moves focus to it; each item links to its field. Fields are labelled above, with a hint under the label when the format matters.

## Parts for forms of your own

The forms are built from parts you can use in a form of your own.

- `useFormFieldIds({ id, describedBy, hint, error })` returns the ids that tie a field together: `control` for the label's `htmlFor`, `label`, `hint`, `error`, and `describedBy`, which joins the control's own `aria-describedby` with the hint and the error while they show.
- `FormFieldCaption` is the top of every buttery field: the label and, under it, a hint, given `htmlFor`, `label`, `hint` and `hintId`.
- `FormFieldError` is the error under a control, with an icon so it doesn't rest on color alone. It describes the control, and with `announce` it reads a new error out politely with the field's `name` in front, as in "Email: Enter an email address".
- `useAuthForm(values, check, onSubmit, failed)` runs a form's submit: `check` returns the fields that need fixing, and the hook either shows them, with focus on the summary, or passes the values to `onSubmit` and shows the message it returns, if any. When `onSubmit` throws, it shows `failed` and hands the error to `reportError`, so error tracking still sees it. It returns `problems` for the fields' own errors, `reported` for the summary, `failure`, `pending` and `submit`.
- `AuthErrorSummary` is the summary over the form: a danger callout that's read out, with a link to each field in `form.reported`.
- `AuthProviders` lists other ways in under an "or" rule, a full-width soft button for each of its `providers`, each with a generic icon, never someone's brand mark.
- `looksLikeEmail(value)` is the check the forms use for an email address: something, an @, then something with a dot.
