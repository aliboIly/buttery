A catalog page lays out things to browse, such as add-ons or products: a filter column with a search, categories and checkboxes, beside a featured block and grids of cards. Use `CatalogPage` when people pick from many items by kind.

## The parts

<Cards columns={2}>
<Card icon="wrench" title="The filter column">

`filters` holds a `SearchField`, `CatalogPage.Categories`, a list of links to each category, and a `CatalogPage.Group` for each set of checkboxes, then an action.

</Card>
<Card icon="palette" title="The featured block">

`CatalogPage.Featured` is the catalog's featured block: its title and its items on a tinted surface.

</Card>
<Card icon="blocks" title="Sections">

`CatalogPage.Section` is a titled section of items, such as one category's.

</Card>
<Card icon="package" title="Items">

`CatalogPage.Item` is one thing in the catalog, a plain `Catalog.Card`: its tile, its name as a link, what it is, its offer and its tags.

</Card>
</Cards>

The filter column sits beside the catalog from 900px of the page's column in a shell at least 1100px wide, and above it otherwise. The featured block and each section show their items two to a row from 560px of room.

## Other catalogs

`CatalogPage` is one arrangement of [Catalog](/components/catalog)'s parts. For a shop's product grid, a gallery of templates or an app's add-ons, use `Catalog` on its own: a toolbar with a search, filters, a sort and a grid or list toggle, over cards in a grid, a list or rows. `CatalogPage.Item` takes the card's `variant`, `mediaPosition` and `size` for a look other than its plain one.
