Onboarding is a short tour in a dialog: a scene that morphs between steps, text that crossfades in place, page dots, Back and Next, and confetti once it's finished. Use `Onboarding` the first time someone opens an app or a feature, for three to five steps at most.

## How a tour works

<Cards columns={2}>
<Card icon="file" title="Steps">

Each of the `steps` has a `title`, which names the dialog while it shows, a `body` of a line or two and a scene in `media`, drawn 384×200.

</Card>
<Card icon="palette" title="Shared pieces">

`Onboarding.Shared` marks a piece of a scene that carries over: a piece with the same `id` in the next step's scene is the same piece, moved.

</Card>
<Card icon="monitor" title="The first visit">

With a `storageKey`, the tour opens by itself the first time and, once it's finished or closed, never again.

</Card>
<Card icon="package" title="The last step">

The last step's button finishes the tour, and `onFinish` runs as the confetti flies.

</Card>
</Cards>

The dialog keeps its size as the steps change, and Next and Back carry forward and back arrows.

> **Reduced motion.** The scene and the text change at once, and nothing celebrates.
