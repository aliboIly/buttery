The inspector pattern is the set of parts an editor is made of: a `HeaderBar` with the document's title and actions, the `Stage` the work sits on, and the `Inspector`, a panel of titled sections holding rows of controls. Put them in `AppShell` and an editor works from a 390px phone, where the inspector becomes a sheet, to a wide desktop.

## How the parts fit

Inspector rows are 50px tall, with the label at the start and the control at the end; a slider labels itself with its value badge. A section can offer Reset for its own settings. The stage fits the artwork at its shape into the space it has, and the header's actions fold into a "More" menu when they don't fit.
