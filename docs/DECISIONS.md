| 2026-10-20 | `Sticky` has two variants. | 2026-10-20 | `FloatingLanes` hosts three lanes in the toast band, 24px from the edges and clear of sticky bars; elements registered with the callback ref from `useFloatingObstacle` push a lane out of their way.
